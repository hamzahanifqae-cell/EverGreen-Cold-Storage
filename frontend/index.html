How it works

Product

Built for cold stores, not retail POS.

EverGreen is a merchant panel for facilities that hold customer-owned bags, crates, and weight in chambers — then charge storage rent when goods leave or over a billing period.

Ownership model

Depositors keep ownership of their stock. Your team posts receipts, returns, transfers, and damage adjustments without creating shop sales or purchase inventory. That keeps the cold-store ledger clean and easy to explain to customers.

Operations

The daily flow

One operating loop from the door to the bill.

01

Receive

Post inbound lots with product, packages, weight, and chamber allocation. Stock appears on the dashboard after post.

02

Store

Track stock on hand by lot, chamber occupancy, transfers between locations, and temperature exceptions.

03

Return

Dispatch partial or full lots for the same customer. Available quantity is enforced; no sale invoice is created.

04

Bill & collect

Create a bill from remaining stock, run calculation preview, post, then record payment into cash in hand or bank.

Clarity

Clear answer when someone asks “is this my shop stock?”

No. Cold storage movements never create classic sales or purchases product stock.

Why it matters

Goods stay on the customer’s deposit until returned. That is the core difference from a normal shop POS — and the reason EverGreen exists as a dedicated cold-store panel.
